# New in Streamybox 1.4.0

- Sound over HDMI: Android now recognises the TV as an audio output. The chosen audio output is kept after a restart.
- Picture at startup: over HDMI the box now offers refresh rates of up to 60 Hz only. This stops the screen from staying black at power-on on TVs such as the LG C1.
- Reliable startup: the UFS storage is now reset correctly at power-on. This fixes startups that ended in a reboot into download mode.
- These improvements are part of the system image. They need a reinstall with the 1.4.0 flash kit; an app update is not enough.
